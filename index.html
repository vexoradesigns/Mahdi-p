How a project runs

Four steps, no surprises.

01
Discover
A short call or WhatsApp chat about the business — what it sells, who it’s for, and what the site needs to do.
02
Design
A first look at layout and content within a few days, built around the business’s own photos and details.
03
Build
A fast, mobile-first site — the version most customers will actually open on their phone.
04
Launch
The site goes live and gets handed over, with a plain explanation of how to update it later.
